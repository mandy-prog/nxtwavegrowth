# ResumeIQ — AI-Powered Student Growth Campaign

## 📌 Project Overview

ResumeIQ is a student-focused growth campaign designed to promote a free, 60-minute AI project-building workshop. The goal is to help final-year engineering students build practical AI project experience and strengthen their placement resumes.

The campaign aims to achieve **500 registrations in 7 days** with a total budget of **₹2,000**, using community-driven marketing, student referrals, coding clubs, and short promotional videos.

## 🎯 Objectives

- Achieve at least 500 workshop registrations within seven days.
- Help students explore practical AI project development.
- Promote the workshop through low-cost, student-focused marketing channels.
- Encourage word-of-mouth growth through referrals.
- Track campaign performance and improve results using data-driven decisions.

## ✨ Key Features

### 1. Student Registration
A registration flow that allows students to sign up for the AI workshop and understand its benefits.

### 2. Referral Program
Students can invite friends using unique referral codes. Students who successfully refer two friends unlock an ATS Resume PDF and 30 AI Interview Prompts.

### 3. Class Representative Leaderboard
Tracks verified registrations attributed to Class Representatives and encourages participation through performance-based rewards.

### 4. Coding Club Challenges
Collaborates with college coding clubs to promote the workshop through a practical AI-building challenge.

### 5. WhatsApp Promotion
Uses promotional messages, reminders, and short campaign updates to encourage registrations and workshop attendance.

### 6. Campaign Analytics
Tracks registrations, referral activity, conversion rates, attendance estimates, and campaign spending.

### 7. A/B Testing
Proposes experiments to compare promotional messages, referral incentives, and reminder timings to identify more effective growth strategies.

## 📈 Growth Strategy

The campaign uses four primary acquisition channels:

| Channel | Registration Target | Budget |
|---|---:|---:|
| Class Representative rewards | 230 | ₹1,200 |
| Student referrals | 175 | ₹0 |
| Coding club challenges | 105 | ₹500 |
| Creator videos | 35 | ₹300 |
| **Total** | **545** | **₹2,000** |

The target of 545 registrations provides a buffer above the main goal of 500.

## 🧪 Planned A/B Tests

**Test 1: Promotional Messaging**

- Hypothesis: A resume-focused message will attract more registrations than a message focused only on the free workshop.
- Test: Compare two promotional messages.
- Metric: Click-to-registration conversion rate.

**Test 2: Referral Incentives**

- Hypothesis: Highlighting bonus resume and interview resources will encourage more referrals.
- Test: Compare a basic referral message with one that highlights the rewards.
- Metric: Percentage of registered students completing two referrals.

**Test 3: Reminder Timing**

- Hypothesis: Evening reminders may generate more registrations than afternoon reminders.
- Test: Compare the performance of reminders sent at different times.
- Metric: Registration conversion rate.

These are proposed experiments; results should be recorded after the tests are conducted.

## 💰 Budget

The planned campaign budget is ₹2,000:

- Class Representative rewards: ₹1,200
- Coding club challenge prize: ₹500
- Creator videos: ₹300

The campaign emphasizes organic growth and community-based promotion rather than relying primarily on paid advertising.

## 📊 Success Metrics

The campaign will monitor:

- Total registrations
- Cost per registration
- Click-to-registration conversion rate
- Referral completion rate
- Workshop attendance and show-up rate
- Performance of individual growth channels

The projected attendance is approximately 295 students from 545 registrations, assuming a 54% show-up rate.
