FIELD: What did AI suggest you while building this that you deliberately rejected and why?
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
While building the campaign plan and product portal, I deliberately rejected three major AI suggestions where generic LLM advice conflicted with real-world Indian engineering college behavior:

1. Rejected AI's Paid Ad Budget Split (₹1,200 Meta Ads + ₹500 LinkedIn Ads):
AI initially suggested running Instagram/Facebook lead ads and LinkedIn sponsored messages. I rejected this completely. LinkedIn has a ~₹800/day minimum spend, while cold EdTech webinar ads on Meta take 48+ hours in the learning phase and cost ₹45–₹75 per student signup (meaning ₹2,000 would barely get ~35 registrations). Instead, I allocated ₹1,200 into 6 performance micro-bounties (₹300/₹200/₹100) for 25 final-year Class Representatives (CRs) and ₹500 into 5 college coding club co-hosted sprints. A message posted by a Class CR inside an official final-year placement WhatsApp group carries peer authority, costs ~₹5.21 per signup, and gets read within 15 minutes.

2. Rejected "Certificate of Completion" Positioning & an 8-Field Registration Form:
AI generated generic hype copy ("Master Artificial Intelligence in 60 Minutes & Earn a Free Certificate!") alongside an 8-field form asking for Email, CGPA, LinkedIn URL, and GitHub URL. I rejected both. Final-year students already have dozens of webinar certificates that didn't help in placements; what actually drives them is replacing generic Titanic/Iris projects on their resume. I rewrote the hook around placement resume utility, built an interactive Resume Bullet Previewer on the hero screen, and stripped the form down to 4 mobile-friendly fields + WhatsApp number.

3. Rejected Leaderboard-Only Referrals & Post-Workshop ZIP Code Uploads:
AI suggested a "Top 3 referrers win vouchers" contest and having students upload ZIP files of their code after the workshop. I rejected leaderboard-only referrals because 98% of students know they won't beat campus influencers; instead, I added a guaranteed 2-partner Study-Squad unlock (referring just 2 lab partners unlocks the ATS Resume Prompt Pack immediately). Finally, because 500 students won't zip files at 8 PM on a Sunday, I built an in-browser Automated Rubric Grader (Tab 4) where students paste their Colab link + system prompt at Minute 52 to get an instant score out of 100 and a shareable LinkedIn Proof-of-Build card.
