EXAMPLE 01 / 03 // CHANNEL & BUDGET ALLOCATION

Reallocating ₹2,000 From Cold Paid Ads to Class CR Micro-Bounties

1. Prompt “Get 500 final-year engineering students in India to register for a free 60-minute workshop ('Build Your First AI Project in 60 Minutes') within 7 days on a ₹2,000 budget. Suggest channel split.”
2. AI Suggestion • ₹1,200 on Instagram & Facebook Lead Ads targeting CS students.
• ₹500 on LinkedIn Sponsored Messaging.
• ₹300 on cold emailing college Training & Placement Officers (TPOs).
3. What Was Rejected & Why • LinkedIn requires ~₹800/day minimum spend.
• Cold EdTech webinar CPL on Meta in India is ₹45–₹75 (₹1,200 = ~22 signups).
• TPO cold emails take weeks for approval—too slow for a 7-day window.
4. Final Implementation • ₹1,200 → Class CR Micro-Bounties: 6 performance bounties for 25 final-year CRs sharing tracked links in official class WhatsApp groups (230 regs).
• ₹500 → 5 Coding Club Co-Hosted Sprints: (105 regs).
• ₹0 → 2-Partner Squad Referral Unlock: (175 regs).
EXAMPLE 02 / 03 // POSITIONING & SIGNUP FLOW

Replacing “Free Certificate” Hype With the Resume Bullet Previewer

1. Prompt “Write a hero hook and registration form for final-year B.Tech students for 'Build Your First AI Project in 60 Minutes'.”
2. AI Suggestion • Headline: “Master Artificial Intelligence in 60 Minutes & Get a Free Certificate!”
• Form: 8-field form asking for Name, Email, Phone, College, Branch, CGPA, LinkedIn URL, and GitHub URL.
3. What Was Rejected & Why • Final-year students have 10+ webinar certificates that didn't help in placements.
• Asking for LinkedIn/GitHub URLs on mobile between college lectures causes ~60% form drop-off.
4. Final Implementation • Hook: “Stop putting ‘Titanic CSV’ on your placement resume.”
• Interactive Widget: Branch + Role selector previewing the exact STAR resume bullet unlocked at Minute 60.
• 4-Field Mobile Form: Name, WhatsApp #, College, Batch.
EXAMPLE 03 / 03 // VIRAL MECHANIC & IN-WORKSHOP GRADER

2-Partner Instant Unlock & Automated In-Browser Rubric Grader

1. Prompt “Design a student referral contest and a way to evaluate 500 student AI projects at the end of a 60-minute workshop.”
2. AI Suggestion • “Top 3 students who refer 20+ friends win vouchers.”
• “Have students upload a 5-minute video and a ZIP file of their source code after the session.”
3. What Was Rejected & Why • Top-3-only contests demotivate 98% of regular students.
• Recording videos and zipping code at 8 PM on Sunday guarantees <5% completion.
4. Final Implementation • 2-Partner Squad Unlock: Referring just 2 lab partners unlocks the ATS Resume & 30 AI Prompts Pack immediately.
• Instant Rubric Grader (Tab 4): Audits Colab URL + system prompt across 4 criteria and mints a LinkedIn Proof-of-Build Card.
